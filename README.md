# CarePlus Hospital — Digital Patient Intake Transformation & Gap Analysis

**Case study:** CarePlus Hospital Network (Project Pulse, Healthcare/HealthTech)
**Project type:** Discovery, process improvement and requirements — business analysis portfolio case study
**Status note:** Analytical proposal built from a supplied case brief, not an implemented hospital system. Baseline figures are from the case brief; targets and plans are the analyst's own recommendations.

## Business context
CarePlus Hospital Network, a 20+ year regional provider with strong clinical expertise, was losing patients to faster-moving urgent-care competitors because of its manual, paper-based front-desk registration process.

## Problem
- Patients complete **four-page paper forms** by hand
- Registration can take **up to 2 hours**
- **15% medical-record error rate** from manual transcription
- Nurses spend **40% of their shift** typing forms instead of delivering care
- Untrained front-desk staff struggle to troubleshoot frozen iPads where digital tools exist
- Older patients may be unfamiliar with tablet-based check-in

## Approach
Applied a structured BA toolkit to move from problem to a feasible transformation path:
- **As-Is / To-Be swimlane process mapping** — current paper-and-retype workflow vs. proposed mobile pre-registration / assisted iPad check-in integrated with the existing EHR
- **People–Process–Technology (PPT) gap analysis** across people, process, technology, data/compliance, and measurement
- **Fishbone root-cause analysis** of registration delays, data errors, and nursing overload
- **SWOT, McKinsey 7S, and PESTLE** assessments of organizational readiness and external factors
- **Stakeholder analysis** across patients, front-desk/nursing, clinicians, IT/EHR admin, hospital management, privacy/compliance, and vendor
- **Business Requirements (BRD) summary** — 6 business requirements (BR-01–BR-06) with proposed acceptance measures
- **Functional & non-functional requirements** — 7 functional requirements (FR-01–FR-07) plus security, availability, performance, accessibility, interoperability, and privacy requirements
- **Risk register** with 6 prioritized risks and mitigations (EHR integration failure, unauthorized access, patients unable to self-register, staff resistance, device/network outage, scope creep)
- **Phased implementation roadmap** — Discovery & Design (wks 1–3) → Prototype & Integration Build (wks 4–7) → Controlled Pilot & Training (wks 8–9) → Review & Phased Expansion (wks 10–12) → Continuous Improvement (ongoing)
- **KPI and benefits measurement plan** tying targets back to the case baseline

## Recommended solution
Secure mobile pre-registration or assisted on-site iPad check-in, integrated with CarePlus's existing EHR system — designed around one-time data capture, duplicate-record checks, assisted service for patients who need it, human review of exceptions (not silent overwrites), and operational fallback for network/device/EHR downtime. The priority gap flagged: a secure, reliable EHR integration — without it, a digital form would just become a new front end to the same manual re-entry problem.

## Proposed targets (vs. case baseline)
| KPI | Case baseline | Proposed target |
|---|---|---|
| Registration time | Up to 2 hours (max reported) | ≤10 minutes, once stabilized |
| Medical record error rate | 15% reported | <2% target |
| Nursing time spent typing forms | 40% of shift reported | Reduced — measured via pre/post time study |
| Patient satisfaction | Not provided | Baseline set; ≥4.5/5 target if adopted |

*These are proposed analyst targets to be validated with sponsors against a measured baseline — not confirmed or achieved results.*

## Outcome / Impact
A structured, evidence-based case for moving CarePlus from paper intake to a digitally supported, EHR-integrated workflow — with the gap analysis, requirements, risks, and phased roadmap needed to take the recommendation to a discovery and feasibility decision. The report is explicit that digitization alone does not fix the bottleneck: the core fix is a verified, auditable data flow between intake and EHR, not just a new paper form.
